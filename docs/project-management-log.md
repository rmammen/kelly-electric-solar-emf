# Project Management Log

I maintained this running log throughout the semester as the shared record of stakeholder meetings, decisions, dependencies, blockers, and next steps. Entries correspond to our weekly Monday class meetings. Client-confidential details have been omitted or generalized.

---

**Feb 9** — Team assigned to work with Kelly Electric on EMF interactions with solar arrays.

**Feb 16** — Designated team liaison for stakeholder communication. Still waiting on a primary Kelly Electric contact and an informational meeting. Introduced to GIS modeling as a possible fallback if data collection is delayed.

**Feb 23** — Derek Newman established as primary Kelly Electric contact. Held our first stakeholder meeting this week, realized the stakeholder group hadn't met in some time until we reached out. Site visitation for panel installation set for March 10. Connected with David Brosch, a solar advocate on the project.

**Mar 2** — David Brosch assigned to select viable panel locations. Confirmed and scheduled an individual meeting with Kelly Electric project managers Derek Newman and John Arnold. Clarified that our team's role is data analysis; site-selection and broader project direction belong to the solar advocates (David Brosch, Steve Skolnik, Albert Nunez). Learned preliminary data must be gathered by March 31 per grant requirements. Panel computers accessible via TeamViewer.

**Mar 9** — Received site-selection criteria from Albert Nunez. Completed Sprint 2 reporting. Focus this week: selecting viable panel locations for county approval. Confirmed next week is a scheduled break from project work due to the site visit on 3/10.

**Mar 12** (site visit, 3/10) — Three sites chosen and staked. BGE wasn't present at the visit; Miss Utility still needed to place ground rods before full installation. Four panels planned, six-month collection period. Discussed a follow-up call and next steps for testing panels before deployment.

**Mar 23** — Update from David Brosch: three trailers being tested on-site, generation data expected later in the week. Learned the Kelly Electric/MEA contract runs through June 30, limiting solar-generation data collection to a 3-month window. Followed up with Derek Newman requesting site photos.

**Mar 30** — Data collection needed to start this week per grant requirements. Raised a scope concern with David Brosch about the team being pulled into site-selection work outside our defined role. Derek Newman added the team to TeamViewer ahead of data collection starting. March 31 confirmed as the latest possible data-collection start date. Delivered our mid-semester check-in presentation.

**Apr 6** — Sprint 4. Received detailed site photos. Began planning what to ask Derek Newman about data collection and analysis, and what the team would do in the absence of complete data. Attempted contact with John Arnold (secondary contact). Received Splashtop access but had ongoing issues actually connecting to the remote data system.

**Apr 8–10** — Provided an interim live-monitoring link (generation, power usage, battery charge) while full access issues persisted. After repeated unanswered emails, called Kelly Electric directly to reach Derek Newman and scheduled a check-in call. On the call (Apr 10), gained access to the VRM portal and learned that Kelly Electric's role was primarily to provide data, while the solar advocates set overall project goals and direction.

**Apr 13** — Met with the "Green Team" to finalize the study's purpose, the three test sites, the core analysis questions, and the planned data sources (VRM for generation, a separate source for EMF) and visualization approach.

**Apr 20** — Guidance received to use dashboard-level data. Began drafting a statement of work for future capstone teams. EMF data still delayed, with a possible partial delivery via thumb drive. Began capturing PV-yield screenshots for each site as available data.

**Apr 27** — Confirmed final presentation date. Data-access issues persisted. Determined the final presentation would emphasize the groundwork and organization completed rather than analytical findings, since the full data set could not be collected and accessed in time. Held our second-to-last stakeholder meeting ahead of the May 1 final presentation, and finalized the closing slide deck.
