# Data Access & Blockers

The project ran against a hard external constraint: Kelly Electric's MEA grant required preliminary data collection to begin by **March 31, 2026**. Everything upstream of that deadline — site installation, remote-system access, and EMF data delivery — had to come together in time, and none of it did smoothly.

## Timeline of Blockers

| When | Blocker | What Happened |
|---|---|---|
| Mar 10 | Site installation | Site visit to implant panels; BGE wasn't present; Miss Utility still needed to place ground rods before installation could finish |
| Mar 23–30 | Installation delay | Trailers were still being tested on-site; photos of the completed installation were still pending as the March 31 deadline approached |
| Apr 6 | Remote access | Received Splashtop access but had ongoing issues actually connecting to the remote data-collection software |
| Apr 8 | Partial workaround | Provided a live monitoring link as an interim fix, giving visibility into generation, power usage, and battery charge — but not full system access |
| Apr 6–9 | Contact unresponsive | Repeated attempts to reach Derek Newman for a check-in meeting went unanswered by email; I called Kelly Electric directly to get his number rather than keep waiting |
| Apr 10 | Access resolved (partial) | Reached Derek Newman by phone, held a check-in call, and gained access to the VRM portal for solar-generation data |
| Apr 13–27 | EMF data | The separate EMF measurement data remained delayed through the rest of the project; requested clarification on which variables were actually needed, without full resolution |

## The Contingency Plan

Data delays weren't a surprise risk — GIS modeling had been raised as early as February as a fallback in case data collection stalled. When it became clear in April that we might not get complete data in time, I pushed the team to explicitly plan for that scenario rather than waiting it out:

- Identified dashboard-level VRM data as the most reliable data source available
- Considered a partial approach — analyzing EMF data delivered on a thumb drive up to whatever date it was available
- Began drafting a statement of work so that whatever we couldn't finish would be usable by the next capstone team

## Outcome

By April 27, with EMF data still delayed and too many collection/access obstacles to work through in the time remaining, the team determined a full analysis wasn't achievable this semester. That decision is covered in [Project Timeline](05-project-timeline.md).
